There is something deeply satisfying about working with paper. In our digital world of keyboards and touchscreens, spending a quiet afternoon cutting, pasting, and arranging organic materials offers a tactile creative outlet.

In this guide, we will look at three simple paper crafts you can do at home with minimal supplies: pressed flower cards, handmade journal pages, and aesthetic flatlay setups.

## 1. The Art of Pressing Flowers

Pressed flowers are a beautiful way to bring nature into your craft projects. They add vintage, botanical charm to any paper surface.

*   **Gathering:** Pick small, thin flowers and leaves. Violas, pansies, clover, and baby's breath are perfect because they lie flat.
*   **The Press Setup:** Open a heavy textbook. Place a sheet of plain parchment paper on a page. Lay your flowers down, ensuring they do not touch. Cover them with another sheet of parchment, and close the book.
*   **Waiting:** Place a heavy object on top of the book. Leave the flowers undisturbed for two weeks until they are completely dry and paper-thin.

## 2. Creating Pressed Flower Cards

Once your flowers are dry, you can mount them onto heavy watercolor cardstock to create greeting cards or art prints.

Mix a tiny drop of PVA glue with water. Use a fine toothpick to apply a minute dot of glue to the back of the dried flower. Gently press the flower onto your card using tweezers. 

To protect the flower and keep it from peeling, you can paint a very thin layer of diluted glue over the top, sealing it to the paper. Let it dry completely.

## 3. Styling Aesthetic Journal Pages

If you keep a sketchbook or bullet journal, arranging paper scraps is a wonderful way to document your ideas. Mix textures and styles:

*   **Torn Edges:** Instead of using scissors, tear the edges of kraft paper or vintage book pages. The textured, fibrous edge adds visual interest.
*   **Layering:** Layer a piece of torn brown paper, a scrap of music sheet, a pressed green leaf, and your handwritten notes on top of each other.
*   **Fine Outlines:** Draw a delicate border around your layered paper collage using a fine black pen to tie the entire composition together.
*   **Washi Tape:** Use muted, solid-colored washi tape (in cream, sage, or blush) to secure elements in place while keeping the layout elegant and clean.
